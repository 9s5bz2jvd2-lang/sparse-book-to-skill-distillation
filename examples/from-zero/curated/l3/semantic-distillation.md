# L3: chunk-complete semantic distillation

## Honest division of labor

Python can ingest, chunk, hash, template, validate, and build deterministic indexes. It cannot understand arbitrary source meaning. An agent or human must read every complete chunk and author the semantic contract.

## Per-chunk requirements

- concise chunk summary;
- reusable knowledge nodes or a reviewed no-reusable-knowledge reason;
- L0 weighted triggers, expert anti-triggers, and one-line purpose;
- L1 brief, safety red lines, and uncertainty;
- L2 workflow and escalation conditions;
- L3 module path with substantive content;
- risk tags and claim-level chunk/source/line provenance;
- global anti-trigger notes where the source/domain boundary requires them.

The work queue and source map must agree with every record. A complete node must cite lines inside a known chunk, and every manifest chunk must be cited or explicitly marked as containing no reusable knowledge.
